# Rules: no AI look, a real hand

Paste these into every brief as hard rules.

## Never

- Soft airbrushed glows, gaussian blooms, smooth gradients and gradient meshes, bokeh, lens flares, "cinematic" blur, photoreal rendering.
- Clone-stamped repetition: every window, head, cobble, leaf cluster, wave and person is varied.
- Perfect symmetry, ruler-perfect perspective, perfect circles, perfectly parallel hatching.
- Generic stock figures, emoji-like faces, clip-art landmarks (the famous monument of the city), folk or tourist motifs used as decoration.
- Imperfection as a filter on top (noise layer, "grunge" overlay, displacement on a finished image). Imperfection must come from **how each mark is made**.
- Image-generation models, traced photos, downloaded art.
- Text the brief didn't ask for. No brand names.
- Noise: confetti, specks, scattered fragments, hairline strokes, dots used as texture. Marks are decisions, not static.
- Shapes nobody can identify. If a viewer can't tell what a shape is from arm's length, cut it or fix it.
- Wrong colours on iconic objects (a city's auto, cab, metro): those keep their true colours. Everything else is a designed palette, free but harmonious.

## What a real hand does (build these in)

- **Pressure**: a stroke swells and thins along its length, starts with a small blob and lifts off with a taper.
- **Wobble and tremor**: the path is jittered at its control points and has a slow tremor along it, more on long strokes.
- **Overshoot and fall-short**: corners are drawn edge by edge; lines cross past corners or don't quite meet.
- **Re-drawn passes**: now and then part of a line is drawn again, slightly offset and thinner.
- **Closed shapes don't close perfectly**: they stop short or run past the start.
- **Fills are loose**: spot colour sits slightly off the line, overflows here, leaves a gap there. It's a separate screen, misregistered.
- **Hatching by hand**: uneven spacing, uneven angle, strokes of different lengths, some doubled.
- **Thinning out**: the drawing loses detail object by object toward its edges and into the distance, as if the artist stopped drawing. No rectangular frame.
- **Choice of detail**: lavish detail at the focal point, economy elsewhere. A hand does not draw everything at the same density.
- **Character**: every person has a gesture, a posture, clothes, hair, an accessory, and is readable as a silhouette.

## Light without glows

Flat shapes of light; rays as brush strokes; broken concentric rings; coarse hand-made halftone; dry-brush; hatching that gets denser toward the source; carved lines (linocut); light pooled on the ground as a flat shape; reflections as stacked broken dashes.

## Print honesty

Each ink is its own layer, with its own slight misregistration, ink spread on the edges, and grain decided per ink. Overprint versus knockout is a deliberate choice. Binary alpha. See OUTPUTS.md.
